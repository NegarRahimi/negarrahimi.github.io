I also contributed to a project investigating the use of transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS) in individuals with multiple sclerosis.

Beyond academia, I gained valuable industry experience as a Research and Development Engineer, developing skills in product innovation, team coordination, and operational efficiency.
